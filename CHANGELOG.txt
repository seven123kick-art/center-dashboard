Version6 D4-16 / Sync Failure Visibility
Date: 2026-08-17

PURPOSE
- Do not silently treat Cloud sync failures as complete success after local save/delete succeeds.
- Distinguish local completion from Cloud reflection for the user.

CHANGES
- Monthly PL import: warn when background Cloud sync fails; warn when old preliminary cleanup fails after confirmed import.
- Capacity import: warn when Cloud sync fails after local save.
- Landing forecast daily actuals: warn when Cloud sync fails after import/delete.
- Delivery list PDF / route payment import: warn when Cloud sync fails after local save.
- Field detail full reset: warn when Cloud sync fails after local deletion.
- {ok:false} sync results are also treated as failures.
